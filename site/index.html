Aion 2 Calc

A live damage meter with an in-game overlay, combat log reviews, build optimization and a raid planner for AION 2. Free and open source.

Download the app Browse combat logs Open the Raid Planner

Combat logs, leaderboards, timers and the raid planner work right here in your browser. The desktop app adds the live meter and overlay, build optimization and gear advice.

Live meter & overlay

Party DPS, healing and damage taken as you fight, with an in-game overlay that starts fresh each pull and shows boss HP, cooldowns and the break bar.

Combat log reviews

Every fight is saved: per-skill damage, buffs, crowd control, the timeline and your parse against the community's runs. Share a log with one link.

Builds & gear

Best skills, stigmas, Daevanion, arcana and stat priority for any class, a copy-paste macro, and gear advice from your own character and fights.

Community logs

Public combat logs, leaderboards, completed-run speed, boss progression and class stats from everyone who shares their fights.

Raid planner

Drop players, enemies, markers and AoE zones on a map, scrub a timelapse to plan movement, lay out party buffs and publish the plan.

Timers & news

Field and world boss timers for your region, recorded boss defeats, and the official AION 2 news from Global, Korea and Taiwan.
