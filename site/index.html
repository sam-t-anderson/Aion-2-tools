aion2calc

Plan your AION 2 build, learn from your fights, and plan raids on a visual timelapse map — free and open source.

Open the Raid Planner Download the app

The raid planner runs right here in your browser — no install. The desktop app adds build optimization, gear advice and combat-log analysis.

Build & rotation optimizer

Best skills, stigmas, Daevanion, arcana, stat priority, a copy-paste macro and a priority rotation for any class — in the desktop app.

Combat-log analyzer

Paste an AbyssLogs link for an AionFlex-style breakdown: per-skill share, crit/double/perfect, cooldown use, timeline and idle time, compared with the optimum.

Raid & boss planner

Drop player, enemy, marker and AoE tokens on a map, scrub a timelapse slider and drag each to plan movement over time. Lay out party buffs; overlay a real fight.
